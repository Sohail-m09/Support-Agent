doc_id: shipping_times_01
category: shipping

Standard delivery takes 3 to 7 business days in metro cities and 5 to 10 business days elsewhere. Express delivery takes 1 to 3 business days in selected cities.

---

doc_id: shipping_fees_01
category: shipping

Standard shipping is free for orders above ₹499. For orders of ₹499 or less, the shipping fee is ₹49. Express delivery costs ₹99 extra.

---

doc_id: shipping_tracking_01
category: shipping

A tracking link is sent by SMS and email once the order is shipped. If the tracking status has not changed for 3 days, the customer can contact support and we will check with the delivery partner.

---

doc_id: shipping_delay_01
category: shipping

If a parcel is more than 5 business days later than the promised delivery date, the customer can wait or cancel the order for a full refund. Support will also ask the delivery partner for an update.

---

doc_id: shipping_address_01
category: shipping

The delivery address can be changed only before the order is shipped. After shipping, the address cannot be changed, and the customer must contact the delivery partner directly.
