doc_id: cancel_before_shipping_01
category: cancellations

An order can be cancelled for free at any time before it is shipped. The full amount is refunded to the original payment method, following the normal refund timelines.

---

doc_id: cancel_after_shipping_01
category: cancellations

An order cannot be cancelled once it is shipped. The customer can refuse the delivery, or return the item after delivery under the return policy for that product category.
