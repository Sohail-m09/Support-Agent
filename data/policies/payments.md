doc_id: cod_policy_01
category: payments

Cash on Delivery (COD) is available for orders up to ₹5,000. A COD fee of ₹40 applies. Orders above ₹5,000 must be paid online.

doc_id: gift_cards_01
category: payments

Gift cards cannot be returned or refunded for cash. They are valid for 12 months from the purchase date. If an order paid with a gift card is refunded, the amount goes back to the gift card balance.

---

doc_id: payment_failed_01
category: payments

If money was deducted but the order was not placed, the amount is refunded automatically within 5 to 7 business days. If it is not refunded after 7 business days, the customer should send the transaction ID to support.
