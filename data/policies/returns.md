doc_id: returns_electronics_01
category: returns

Electronics (earbuds, speakers, smartwatches, power banks) can be returned within 10 days of delivery. The item must be unused, with the original box, charger, and all accessories. Pickup is free. The refund is issued after the returned item passes inspection.

---

doc_id: returns_clothing_01
category: returns

Clothing and footwear can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unworn and unwashed, with all tags attached. Pickup is free. Innerwear and socks cannot be returned for hygiene reasons.

---

doc_id: returns_home_01
category: returns

Home and kitchen items (lamps, bedsheets, mugs, mixer grinders) can be returned within 15 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in the original packaging. Large appliances are picked up by our delivery partner within 3 business days of the return request.

---

doc_id: returns_beauty_01
category: returns

Beauty and personal care items can be returned within 7 days of delivery, only if they are sealed and unopened. Opened items cannot be returned, unless they arrived damaged or are the wrong item.

---

doc_id: returns_how_to_01
category: returns

To start a return, send the order number and the reason to support. We will arrange a pickup within 2 business days. Keep the item packed with its accessories until pickup. A return request is not a refund: the refund is decided after inspection.

---

doc_id: returns_non_returnable_01
category: returns

These items cannot be returned: gift cards, custom-made or personalised items, innerwear, perishable goods, and items marked Final Sale on the product page.
