doc_id: damaged_on_arrival_01
category: damaged

If an item arrives damaged, report it within 7 days of delivery with clear photos. The customer can choose a replacement or a full refund, including the shipping fee. Pickup is free.

---

doc_id: defective_after_use_01
category: damaged

If an item stops working after the return window has ended, it is handled under warranty, not as a refund. Support will guide the customer to a repair or replacement. Items that stop working within the return window can follow the normal return policy.

---

doc_id: wrong_item_01
category: damaged

If the wrong item is delivered, report it within 7 days of delivery. We pick up the wrong item for free and send the correct item or give a full refund, including the shipping fee.

---

doc_id: missing_item_01
category: damaged

If an item or accessory is missing from the parcel, report it within 3 days of delivery with a photo of the package and the shipping label. After verification, we send the missing item or refund its price.

---

doc_id: damaged_evidence_01
category: damaged

For damaged, wrong, or missing item claims, the customer must share the order number and clear photos of the item, the packaging, and the shipping label. If photos are missing or unclear, the assistant should ask for them once, then pass the case to a human agent.
