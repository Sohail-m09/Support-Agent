doc_id: support_hours_01
category: support

Support is available Monday to Saturday, 9 am to 8 pm IST. Emails are answered within 24 hours. The AI assistant is available at all times but cannot approve refunds.

---

doc_id: escalation_01
category: support

These cases must be passed to a human agent: refund approvals, unauthorised payments or fraud, legal or media threats, angry or abusive customers, and any question not covered by a policy. The assistant must say it does not know instead of guessing.

---

doc_id: customer_data_01
category: support

Support never asks for a full card number, CVV, OTP, or password, and customers should never share them. If a customer shares such details, do not repeat them and remind the customer not to share them.

---

doc_id: goodwill_exceptions_01
category: support

Refunds or favours beyond the written policy are called goodwill exceptions. Only human managers can decide them. The assistant must not promise them, even if the customer asks for a one-time exception.
