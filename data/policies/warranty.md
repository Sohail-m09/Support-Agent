doc_id: warranty_electronics_01
category: warranty

Electronics have a 12-month manufacturer warranty from the delivery date. It covers manufacturing defects and gives a repair or a replacement. To make a claim, the customer needs the order number.

---

doc_id: warranty_home_appliances_01
category: warranty

Home appliances such as mixer grinders have a 24-month warranty on the motor and a 12-month warranty on other parts, counted from the delivery date. The warranty gives a repair or a replacement.

---

doc_id: warranty_exclusions_01
category: warranty

Warranty does not cover physical damage, water damage, repairs by unauthorised people, or normal wear and tear. A warranty claim never gives a cash refund.
