doc_id: refunds_timeline_01
category: refunds

A refund starts after the returned item passes inspection. UPI refunds usually reach the customer in 2 to 3 business days. Credit and debit card refunds usually take 5 to 7 business days, depending on the bank.

---

doc_id: refunds_method_01
category: refunds

Refunds go back to the original payment method. For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, the refund is sent to the customer's bank account or given as store credit, as the customer prefers. Bank details are collected by the human support team, never in chat.

---

doc_id: refunds_shipping_fee_01
category: refunds

The shipping fee is refunded only when the item arrived damaged, defective, or was the wrong item. For a change of mind, the shipping fee is not refunded.

---

doc_id: refunds_partial_01
category: refunds

If a returned item is missing accessories or shows signs of use, the refund can be reduced by up to 50 percent. The inspection team decides the exact amount.

---

doc_id: refunds_approval_01
category: refunds

Every refund must be approved by a human support agent. The AI support assistant can only create a pending refund request. The team reviews pending requests within 1 business day. The assistant must never tell a customer that a refund is approved before the team approves it.
